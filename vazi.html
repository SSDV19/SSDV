01 / OVERVIEW

What is VAZI?

VAZI is an AI-powered outing planning platform built specifically for Chennai. It takes your mood, budget, available time, group size, and preferred area — and generates a complete multi-venue itinerary with real-time distances, curated cost breakdowns, and a live interactive route map. Think of it as a smart travel agent in your browser, calibrated for Chennai life.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Step-by-Step Working

Here's how VAZI takes you from zero to a fully planned outing in seconds.

STEP 01

Set Your Outing Preferences

Everything starts with understanding what kind of outing you want. VAZI asks you just four simple questions to build your perfect plan.

  • Outing Purpose — Date Night, Friends Hangout, Family Outing, or Solo Explorer
  • Distance Preference — Nearby Hangout (<5km), Moderate Drive (5–15km), or Long Drive (ECR/Highway)
  • Total Budget — Pick from ₹500 to ₹5000 per person, with quick-select chips
  • Available Duration — Set how many hours you have (slider-based)
  • Start Time & Group Size — So timings and totals are personalized
  • Preferred Area & Transport Mode — Car, Bike, or Cab/Auto
STEP 02

AI Generates Your Personalized Itinerary

Hit "Generate AI Outing Itinerary" and VAZI's geospatial AI engine kicks in. In seconds, it constructs a complete multi-venue plan optimized for distance, budget, crowd, and mood.

  • AI selects 3–5 venues that fit your purpose (café → cinema → restaurant → dessert)
  • Each venue is sequenced to minimize travel distance between stops
  • Timing is auto-calculated: arrival, recommended stay, and departure per leg
  • Per-person and total costs computed with curated menu highlights
  • Natural-language AI Planning Reasoning explains why each venue was chosen
  • AOI Outing Health Score (0–100) evaluates budget, traffic, crowd, mood, safety & more
STEP 03

Browse Venues & Swap Freely

Don't like a venue? No problem. Each card in the itinerary is fully interactive — swap any venue while the AI re-optimizes the rest, complete with safety scores and parking status.

  • Each venue card shows category, rating, location, timings, and per-person cost
  • Safety Score (0–100) displayed on every stop — red alert for below-70 after 9 PM
  • Parking Prediction — 🟢 Easy / 🟡 Moderate / 🔴 Full with estimated wait times
  • Time Worthiness Score — 0–100% rating based on crowd, cost, and experience
  • Budget Leak Detector — flags overspend with cheaper equally-rated alternatives
  • Swap button replaces the venue while keeping the rest of the itinerary intact
STEP 04

Review the Full Plan & AI Health Score

Before starting your outing, VAZI gives you a complete bird's-eye view — every venue, every timing, every rupee — plus a master AOI Health Score that evaluates 8 factors at once.

  • AOI Outing Health Score (0–100) — radial gauge dial evaluating Budget, Traffic, Weather, Crowd, Mood, Energy, Time, Safety
  • 🟢 80–100 Excellent · 🟡 60–79 Good · 🟠 40–59 Fair · 🔴 0–39 Poor
  • 1-Click AI Health Rescue — auto-fixes the lowest-scoring factor instantly
  • Full budget utilization shown with a color-coded gradient bar
  • Interactive route map shows all stops plotted with numbered pins
  • One-tap Start Outing to launch live GPS navigation mode
STEP 05

Live GPS Navigation — Leg by Leg

When you're ready to go, VAZI switches into a full live navigation mode. This isn't just a map — it's a real-time outing co-pilot that guides you through each venue stop.

  • Real-time traffic detection via Google Maps Live Matrix ("Smooth flow detected")
  • Current location shown as a cyan "You Are Here" dot on a live Leaflet map
  • Optimal route drawn to the next destination with a dashed purple route line
  • Predicted ETA and Expected Leave time always visible
  • "Should We Leave Now?" Traffic Predictor — compares Next 15 min vs. Next 45 min
  • Weather Auto-Replanning — "Simulate Rain" button replaces outdoor stops with indoor alternatives in real time
  • One-tap Reached! button to check in and proceed to the next leg
03 / AI INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

🧠 15 Predictive AI Features

VAZI's AI Intelligence Engine goes far beyond a simple itinerary generator — it monitors, predicts, adapts, and rescues your outing in real time across 15 intelligent modules.

👥
Crowd Prediction System
Each venue shows hourly crowd curves (4 PM → 10 PM) as live progress bars. AI compares across venues and suggests the least-crowded window — e.g., "Go to Besant Nagar first. Visit Marina after 8 PM. Save 40 minutes."
🧠 Predictive AI
🌧️
Weather Auto-Replanning
When rain is detected mid-outing, AI automatically replaces outdoor stops with indoor alternatives (cafes, arcades, malls, movies). No manual input needed — itinerary rebuilds itself. Try it with the "Simulate Rain" button.
⚡ Live Adaptive
💸
Budget Leak Detector
Tracks real-time spend vs. planned budget. When overspent, AI suggests cheaper alternatives with equal ratings (e.g., Restaurant B ₹420 instead of A ₹700). Shows exact overspend with a red warning banner on the stop card.
🧠 Smart Finance
🦶
Fatigue & Walking Tracker
Monitors cumulative walking distance across all stops. Triggers rest break suggestions after heavy walking (e.g., Elliot's Beach → Marina adds 3.2 km). Recommends seated venues to recover energy.
🧠 Energy AI
😌
Mood Recovery Engine
Detects mood drops mid-trip using energy and satisfaction signals. Suggests micro-recovery activities: chai break, live music, dessert stop. "Simulate Mood Drop" button triggers a live demo.
🧠 Mood Intelligence
🔋
Social Battery Meter
Tracks social energy across the group over time. When social energy dips, AI flags it and suggests quieter venues or a wind-down spot — ensuring no one burns out mid-outing.
👥 Social AI
🛡️
Safety Score & Night Guard
Every venue has a 0–100 Safety Score displayed on its stop card. Below-70 venues after 9 PM trigger a red Night Guard alert with safer alternatives automatically suggested.
🔴 Night Guard
🚗
Parking Prediction
Each stop shows real-time parking status: 🟢 Easy / 🟡 Moderate / 🔴 Full, including estimated search time and nearest parking hubs with distances — so you're never caught off guard.
⚡ Live Status
🚦
"Should We Leave Now?" Traffic Predictor
Decision card on each stop compares traffic in Next 15 min vs. Next 45 min with a clear recommendation: 🟢 Leave now — traffic is better | 🟡 Stay 20 more minutes — it'll ease up.
⚡ Real-time Traffic
⭐
Time Worthiness Score
Each venue gets a 0–100% Time Worthiness rating based on crowd, cost, and experience value. Helps prioritize which stops are worth the time vs. which are safely skippable.
🧠 Value AI
⚖️
Group Consensus Equalizer
Analyzes every group member's preferences and constraints. Finds a fair itinerary that maximizes satisfaction for everyone — not just the loudest voice in the group.
👥 Group Fair AI
🔀
Psychological Energy Flow Optimizer
Sequences stops from High-energy → Medium → Low to avoid energy crashes. Orders your day smartly: Action first, relaxation last — so the outing flows naturally and doesn't burn out early.
🧠 Flow AI
📸
Memory Timeline & Story Generator
After a trip, generates an Instagram Reel–style story card with timestamps, photos, stats, and highlights. Includes a shareable recap with confetti animation — powered by canvas-confetti.
✨ Story Gen
🎪
Unexpected Opportunity Radar
Detects real-time nearby events: street food festivals, concerts, flash pop-ups. Offers a 1-tap Swap to insert the event directly into your live itinerary — no planning required.
⚡ Live Events
🏥
AOI Outing Health Score (0–100)
Master health index evaluating all 8 factors: Budget, Traffic, Weather, Crowd, Mood, Energy, Time, Safety. Displayed as a radial gauge dial. Color-coded tiers: 🟢 80–100 Excellent · 🟡 60–79 Good · 🟠 40–59 Fair · 🔴 0–39 Poor. Includes a 1-Click AI Health Rescue.
🧠 Master Index

Every user gets a unique VAZI ID (e.g., VAZI-8821) — shareable via 1-click copy. Search any friend by typing their VAZI ID (e.g., KEER-4091, AJAY-3301) to connect instantly.
✨ Identity System
🙋
Friend Cards & Status
Friend cards show name, avatar, mood emoji, online status, and last seen. Send friend requests, accept/reject, and invite friends directly to outing groups — accessible via the Friends button in the Navbar.
👥 Social Layer
📨
Request & Invite Flow
Full request lifecycle: send, accept, reject, and invite. Once connected, friends can be added to any outing group instantly — turning a solo plan into a shared experience with one tap.
👥 Social Flow
Example: Your VAZI ID is VAZI-8821. Share it with friends. They search, send a request, you accept — they're in your group and you're planning together in seconds.

Group Chat

06 / MINI-GAMES (ARCADE MODE)

🎮 Playable Mini-Games

VAZI comes with a built-in Arcade Mode — four fully playable games to keep the group entertained before, during, or after the outing.

🤔
Would You Rather
Real multiple-choice dilemmas with live % voting — e.g., 62% vs 38%. Progress bar visualizes the split. Rounds cycle automatically, keeping the group engaged and revealing surprising preferences.
🏆
Travel Quiz Trivia
Questions about Chennai landmarks, travel facts, and street food. Scoring system with streaks, wrong answer tracking, and a Victory Screen 🏆. 10-question rounds with instant right/wrong feedback.
🎡
Truth or Dare Wheel
Spin the wheel to land on Truth or Dare. Curated question and challenge cards per category. The ultimate social ice-breaker for group outings — gets everyone talking and laughing.
📷
AI Photo Challenge
AI gives you a creative photo prompt — e.g., "Capture a reflection in water." Upload your photo → AI scores it aesthetically (0–100). Leaderboard-style challenge for groups that love photography.
